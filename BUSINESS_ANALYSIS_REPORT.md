# BGMI Esports Analytics — Business Analysis

## Executive Summary
This project combines verified flagship BGMI tournament metadata with selected team and player statistics for SQL and Power BI analysis.

## Business Questions
- Which events have the largest prize pools?
- How has the competitive calendar expanded?
- Which teams have championships in the verified event set?
- How much of team scoring comes from eliminations versus placement?
- Which players lead finishes, damage and knocks?
- How do domestic and international events differ?

## Verified Findings
- BGIS 2025 Grand Finals: Team Versatile recorded 169 points across 18 matches; GodLike Esports recorded 152.
- BGIS 2025 MVP leaders included ADMINO, JONATHAN, Spower, AK and ViPER.
- BMPS 2026 overview lists GodLike Esports as champion and Divine Gaming as runner-up.
- BMIC 2025 was won by DRX, with True Rippers second and CAG OSAKA third.
- BGIS 2024 was won by TeamXSpark.

## Limitation
Public sources expose different levels of match/player detail by event. The project separates event metadata from selected verified statistics and does not fabricate unavailable records.
